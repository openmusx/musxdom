Detailed Description

Entry positions and stem-specific geometry for one entry.

Coordinates are upward-positive integer EVPU. All entries in a beam must use the same origin and source-staff scale, with staff-origin offsets and relative scaling already applied. Values and their differences must be representable by int64_t. Supply note positions before reverse-stem adjustments. A caller may translate the embedded vertical positions and reversal together to another common origin; staff-relative positions and source-staff metrics retain their original meanings.
