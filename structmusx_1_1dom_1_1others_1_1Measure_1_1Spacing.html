Detailed Description

Justified horizontal beat spacing for the measure's current width and positioning mode.

Coordinates include integer rounding and need no further stretching. Add musicStart to a slot's offset or endOffset to obtain its position from the measure's left edge. The music area excludes both front/back spacing options and measure extras. Gaps between spans retain their separate endpoints. For proportional spacing there is one span from zero to musicWidth. A chart's final span end may differ from musicWidth; coordinate conversion uses that span end until it is reached, then uses musicWidth for extrapolation.
