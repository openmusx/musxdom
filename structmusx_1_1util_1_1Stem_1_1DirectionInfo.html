Detailed Description

Direction decision and logical assignments for the queried entry.

Inspect resolution as well as primaryDirection. An empty direction in a successful forced query means automatic direction, not missing data. For split/double-stem entries, an effective calculation can resolve a local primary direction even when the beam's geometry remains unresolved.
