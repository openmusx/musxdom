Detailed Description

Calculates accidental display in a note's part, staff, measure, and pitch context.

Results are shared by all note wrappers belonging to the document. The first query evaluates the participating layers of the measure. Call Document::clearAccidentalCache after editing notes, frames, staff settings, key signatures, part voicing, or pitch display settings. Glyph selection, accidental positioning, staff-system visibility, and page clipping are excluded.
