int musx::dom::ShapeDefInstruction::StartGroup::rotation {}

Packed rotation value. In both encodings, value 0 means no rotation. Sign+magnitude trig encoding: bit31=sign of cos(theta), bit30=sign of sin(theta), bits 0..10=|sin(theta)|*1024. Angle-marker encoding: bit29 marks payload interpreted as floor(radians * 100000).
