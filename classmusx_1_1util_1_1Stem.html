Detailed Description

Static utilities for logical entry direction, beam direction, and per-note stem assignments.

Calculations use the entry's requested part, effective staff and layer settings, and part-voicing policy. Cross-staff geometry comes from the document or caller-supplied resolvers. Results describe logical stem choices, not visible stem endpoints or the slope of a manually positioned beam. Calculations do not modify the document or cache caller-dependent results.

Calculates geometric stem direction, including cross-staff placement and beam context.

Excludes rhythmic, entry-freeze, layer, staff-direction, and voice forcing. Applies adjusted extrema, reverse-stem settings, rest-beam compatibility, and part-voicing exclusions. Grace and hidden entries do not contribute extrema. Balanced unbeamed extrema select down; balanced beams use the two-extreme vote, with equal votes selecting down. No eligible positions are reported explicitly.

Parameters
entryValid entry whose direction and assignments are requested.
optionsOptional providers for complete geometry and cross-staff origins.
Returns
Geometric decision and assignments, or an unresolved status with no primary direction.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentIf entry is invalid or supplied geometry has the wrong note count.
dom::integrity_errorIf required staff or option records are missing.

Stem::DirectionInfo musx::util::Stem::calcEffectiveDirection ( const dom::EntryInfoPtr & entry,
const Options & options )
static

Resolves contextual or geometric direction and per-note logical stem assignments.

Uses the first contextual override, otherwise the geometric calculation. Ordinary beam members inherit the group direction; split/double-stem entries retain their local primary direction. Split flags assign notes to up or down stems; a split entry without up-split flags uses double-stem assignments. A resolved override needs no geometry. Visible stem endpoints and manual beam placement are not calculated.

Parameters
entryValid entry whose effective direction and assignments are requested.
optionsOptional geometry providers, used only if a geometric decision is needed.
Returns
Effective entry and beam directions, assignments, and resolution status.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentIf entry is invalid or supplied geometry has the wrong note count.
dom::integrity_errorIf required staff or option records are missing.

Stem::DirectionInfo musx::util::Stem::calcForcedDirection ( const dom::EntryInfoPtr & entry,
const Options & options )
static

Finds the first applicable contextual direction in beam order.

At each entry, checks rhythmic notation, entry freeze, applicable layer freeze, staff forcing, and stored voice/grace direction. Unbeamed split/double-stem entries also use their stored direction. Stops at the first applicable entry; does not populate assignments or consult geometry.

Parameters
entryValid entry whose beam or local override is requested.
optionsAccepted for interface consistency; geometry callbacks are not invoked.
Returns
Selected override, reason, and source entry; Resolved with no primary direction means automatic. An encountered legacy mirror frame produces UnsupportedLegacyContext.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentIf entry is invalid.
dom::integrity_errorIf required staff data is missing.
