Enumerator
EntryOrigin 

From the entry's drawing origin to the symbol's handle (its drawing origin).

NoteheadCenter 

From the horizontal center of the entry's primary notehead column to the horizontal center of the symbol's visible bounds. Noteheads drawn on the opposite side of the stem for a second (Note::upStemSecond, Note::downStemSecond) are not in the column. For a rest, the anchor is the horizontal center of the rest.
