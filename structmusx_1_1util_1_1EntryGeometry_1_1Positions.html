Detailed Description

Positions for one entry, with original note indices preserved.

When resolution is MissingGeometry, known staff-relative and vertical positions remain available. Callers requiring complete chord extrema must check resolution before aggregating. A null note element means excluded by the producer (including part voicing) or replaced by a rest, not missing geometry. calc() uses the source-staff reference line as zero. Callers may translate all vertical positions to another common origin; staff-relative positions and source-staff metrics remain unchanged. Stem-specific adjustments and reversal positions are not included.
