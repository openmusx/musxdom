std::function<Stem::GeometryResult(const dom::EntryInfoPtr&)> musx::util::Slur::Options::geometryResolver

Optional complete stem geometry provider for entries consulted by the slur calculation.

Uses Stem::GeometryResult's decision and geometry contract. Supplied geometry bypasses the staff-origin resolver; NotHandled uses document geometry and staffOriginOffsetResolver. Unavailable produces MissingGeometry when direction requires it. Empty callbacks use document geometry. Resolver exceptions propagate to the caller.

◆ staffOriginOffsetResolver

StaffOriginOffsetResolver musx::util::Slur::Options::staffOriginOffsetResolver

Optional cross-staff origin provider used when resolving automatic contour.

Offsets are downward-positive EVPU before source-staff normalization. NotHandled uses document geometry; Unavailable prevents fallback.
