Detailed Description

Resolves note and rest positions without interpreting stems or beam direction.

Staff-relative positions belong to each note's effective staff. calc() returns vertical positions with the entry's source-staff reference line as zero, with upward-positive integer EVPU in the source staff's scale. Results from entries on different source staves require coordinate conversion before comparison. Part-voicing exclusions are honored; hidden and grace entries are not otherwise filtered. These are notation positions, not glyph bounds or manually adjusted notehead drawing coordinates.
